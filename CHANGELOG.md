# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-16

### Added — a soundboard
- **Missions — Soundboard** compendium: six playlists, three per adventure, 70 clips.
  Import one, and each sound plays for everyone when the GM clicks it (Soundboard
  Only, so nothing plays by itself).
- Designed voices for Dr. Cuca, Lindy, Doctor Bob, Liz Yamato, Earl Brown, and
  DocWagon Expert Team Three (Seth Palatine, Viv Geldhausmann, Hawkeye Kurtz, Shawn
  Ferrer); the read-alouds use the campaign narrator. Stock voices, different from
  Double Exposure's, play Captain Burns, Sgt. Tanner, Marcus Powell, the Futuremen,
  DocWagon dispatch and Gloria Tanaka.
- Sound effects and looping ambience: MindSound Music, Pier 60, the DocWagon garage,
  sirens and firefights, the cockatrice warehouse, Brown's guided blasts.
- Lines are performed (ElevenLabs v4 with direction such as [sighs] and [whispers]) and
  are original text, not the book's boxed text. Malpractice follows the book's default
  mole (Shawn); swapping the mole means rewriting his confession lines.

### Fixed
- **Lindy is the store's clerk, not "Dr. Cuca".** The actor was named "Lindy (a.k.a.
  'Dr. Cuca')" and its bio had him running the clinic. In the book he is a Renraku
  operative behind the music store's counter who screens "patients" for the real Dr.
  Cuca, Dr. Claudio Andrade (p.12). The actor keeps its id and portrait.
- Under the Influence's villains are **Renraku**, not Aztechnology, in the "Filling
  Double Exposure's Gaps" journal.
- **Scenes taken from the Scenes compendium would have lost their walls, doors and
  regions** once real maps add them. The pack build now stores them the way Foundry
  expects (as Queen Euphoria and Double Exposure do), and `npm run validate` checks
  their ids. Pulling edits back from Foundry no longer drops journal page content.

## 0.3.0 — Cast portraits

All 14 Missions NPCs now have custom painterly portraits (square 1024px,
rotation-locked) instead of the mystery-man placeholder.

## 0.2.0 — Scenes

The `mi-scenes` pack is no longer empty: **15 placeholder battlemaps**, one per
act of *Malpractice* (8) and *Under the Influence* (7). Each is a labeled
1600×1200 grid map — swap the background for final map art; the Scene keeps its
grid and navigation name. Generated by `tools/gen-scenes.mjs`.

## 0.1.0 — Missions Phase 1

First release. Two complete *Shadowrun Missions* adventures as SR2E compendia:
**Under the Influence** and **Malpractice** — **23 journal entries** (scene-by-scene
GM text, handouts, legwork) and **16 cast actors** (NPCs and critters), built for
the `sr2e` system.

Not yet included: the battle maps as **Scenes** (`mi-scenes` ships as an empty
placeholder) — planned for a later release.
